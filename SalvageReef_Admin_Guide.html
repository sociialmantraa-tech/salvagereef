SalvageReef Admin Manual

Master Admin Console — Complete Options & Pointers Guide (English & Hinglish)

Official Guide
📌 Overview: This quick reference handbook explains each section of the SalvageReef Master Admin Console in concise, easy-to-understand terms. Use this to quickly train your team, understand workflows, and manage daily scrap operations seamlessly.
1 Executive Overview
Tab: Overview
🇬🇧 English Explanation
The main real-time analytics command center displaying key performance indicators (KPIs) computed directly from the live database.
  • Live Charts: Bidding activity timeline & monthly lot performance.
  • Status Breakdown: Active, completed, and upcoming lots.
  • Leaderboard: Top bidders ranked by bid volume and wins.
🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation
Yeh admin ka main dashboard hai jahan real-time database ka live data aur graphs automatically update hote hain.
  • Live Graphs: Date-wise total bids aur auction performance.
  • Status Donut: Kitne lots active hain aur kitne complete hue.
  • Top Bidders: Sabse zyada bidding karne wale buyers ki list.
2 Sell Scrap Requests
Tab: sell-scrap-requests
🇬🇧 English Explanation
Inquiries submitted by external sellers via the "Sell Your Scrap" form (`/sell-scrap`) wanting to list their materials.
  • Seller Profile: Contact name, phone, email, and GST number.
  • Lot Details: Scrap type, weight/quantity, asking price, yard location & photos.
  • 1-Click Conversion: Instantly convert into a Live Auction or Classified listing.
🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation
Jo factory owners ya scrap sellers apna maal bechna chahte hain (`/sell-scrap` se), unki sari requests yahan aati hain.
  • Seller Details: Naam, mobile number, GST aur factory location.
  • Maal ki Details: Scrap photo, quantity (MT), aur seller ka price.
  • 1-Click Publish: Ek click me is request ko Live Auction bana sakte hain.
3 Add New Product / Lot
Tab: add-product
🇬🇧 English Explanation
Direct creation portal for admins to list new public auctions, confidential private tenders, or classified items.
  • Pricing & EMD: Set starting price, reserve price, bid step, and EMD amount.
  • Schedule: Precise start date/time and ending countdown timer.
  • Media Upload: Multiple high-res photos with auto-WebP compression.
🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation
Admin yahan se direct naya Auction Lot ya Private Tender create karke website par live karta hai.
  • Price & EMD: Starting price, minimum bid increase step aur EMD set karein.
  • Timing: Auction kab shuru hoga aur kab countdown khatam hoga.
  • Photos: Multiple scrap photos upload karne ki suvidha.
4 Categories & Locations
Tab: categories-locations
🇬🇧 English Explanation
Master registry of scrap industry categories and supported states/cities across India.
  • Categories: Add or modify categories (e.g. Copper, Heavy Steel, Machinery, Batteries).
  • Locations: Configure target industrial hubs (Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad, Delhi, etc.).
🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation
Scrap categories aur business locations (States & Cities) manage karne ki master table.
  • Categories: Nayi scrap category (Copper, Steel, Machinery) add ya delete karein.
  • Locations: Naye cities aur states add karein jahan auction available hai.
5 Buyer Tender Requests
Tab: approvals
🇬🇧 English Explanation
Gatekeeper approvals desk for locked Private Tenders and confidential corporate lots.
  • Buyer Access: Review corporate buyers requesting permission to view & bid.
  • Approve Access: Grants instant permission to unlock the live bidding room.
  • Reject / Revoke: Prevents unverified bidders from accessing confidential lots.
🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation
Private Tender ya confidential auctions me bid karne ke liye buyers jo entry permission mangte hain, unko yahan se control karte hain.
  • Buyer Approval: Buyer ki company verify karke use bidding room ka access dein.
  • Approve Button: Click karte hi buyer ke liye bidding screen unlock ho jayegi.
  • Reject Button: Anjaan ya fake buyers ko private tender se bahar rakhein.
6 Live Bids & Moderation
Tab: bid-approvals
🇬🇧 English Explanation
Real-time log of every monetary bid placed in live auctions. Bids are accepted automatically for speed, with admin safety moderation.
  • Audit Trail: See exact amount, bidder name, company, and timestamp.
  • Fraud/Typo Prevention: Reject/cancel accidental or rogue bids instantly.
  • Undo Support: 1-click restore if a bid was mistakenly rejected.
🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation
Live auction me aane wali har ek bid ka live log. Bids automatically accept ho jati hain taaki timer smooth chale, par admin ke paas cancel karne ka control rehta hai.
  • Live Monitoring: Kaunsa buyer kitne rupaye ki bid kis second par laga raha hai.
  • Reject Option: Agar kisi ne galti se extra zero laga diya ya fake bid mari, toh use cancel karein.
  • Undo Button: Galti se reject ho gayi bid ko wapas approve karne ki suvidha.
7 Auction Lots & Top 3 Winners (H1/H2/H3)
Tab: auctions
🇬🇧 English Explanation
Complete inventory of all auctions with automated winner determination (Highest Bidder 1, 2, and 3).
  • Winner Ranking: Automatically identifies H1 (Highest), H2 (Runner-up), and H3.
  • Award Contract: Officially award the lot to H1 or fallback to H2 if H1 defaults.
  • Lifecycle Controls: Pause, end immediately, or extend countdown time.
🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation
Sabhi auctions ki main list jahan auction khatam hone par top 3 highest bidders (H1, H2, H3) automatically calculate hote hain.
  • H1 / H2 / H3: Sabse unchi bid (H1) aur doosri unchi bid (H2) automatically dikhti hai.
  • Award Winner: Auction jeetne wale (H1) ko deal confirm karke award karein.
  • Timer Control: Auction ka time badhana ya turant close karna.
8 Classifieds Manager
Tab: classifieds
🇬🇧 English Explanation
Moderation console for fixed-price B2B scrap and surplus machinery listings posted on the classifieds board.
  • Listing Review: Verify price, contact, material specifications, and images.
  • Status Toggle: Approve, reject, delete, or mark as Sold.
🇮🇳 Hinglish Explanation
Fixed-price scrap aur purani machinery ke Classified ads ko approve, edit ya delete karne ka section.
  • Listing Review: Seller ka price, description aur photo check karein.
  • Status: Approved, Pending ya Sold mark karein.
